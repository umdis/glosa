# Demo video script (3 minutes)

The video shows only what was built during the hackathon. Record it on the deployed demo with the
sermons the church allowed us to publish. Spoken narration in English, with the Spanish on screen
subtitled. Target length: 2:50.

| Time      | On screen                                                                                                                                      | Narration                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| --------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 0:00–0:15 | A long sermon playing; a hand scrolling through hours of video.                                                                                | "Our church has years of sermons on video. Finding what was said about one topic, at the exact minute, is impossible. And AI chat makes it worse: it summarizes confidently and cites the wrong minute."                                                                                                                                                     |
| 0:15–0:30 | Home page: _Glosa — Turn footage into footnotes_. The collection as a table of contents.                                                       | "Glosa turns footage into footnotes. A glosa is the note a scribe wrote in a manuscript's margin. Here, the margin is where the evidence lives."                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| 0:30–0:55 | Type «tentaciones». Concordance results; click a red folio, the video jumps to 3:58.                                                           | "Search by what was said. Spanish word forms are understood: 'tentaciones' finds 'tentación'. Every red folio is a verified moment: click it and the video plays from there."                                                                                                                                                                                |
| 0:55–1:35 | Ask: _¿Qué premio recibe quien soporta la tentación?_ Show «Searching the transcripts…», then claims with sealed quotes. Click «James… 14:03». | "Ask a question. Muse Spark runs as an agent: it searches several times with different words, reads the context, and answers in claims with verbatim quotes. Then our server checks every quote against the transcript and anchors it to the real minute. The model never writes a timestamp: when we measured, it was off by one or two minutes on speech." |
| 1:35–1:50 | Ask: _¿Qué se dijo sobre la astronomía?_ → _no evidence_.                                                                                      | "And when the videos don't talk about something, Glosa says so instead of inventing."                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| 1:50–2:15 | Video page of the parenting sermon at 32:03: the whiteboard appears as a boxed note in the transcript. Then the Scripture index.               | "Muse Spark also reads the screen, and here it is exact to the second: whiteboards and slides land in the transcript when they appear. Every Bible passage, spoken or shown, is checked against the real chapters and verses: James 1:12 was said eight minutes after naming the book, and Glosa still found it."                                            |
| 2:15–2:40 | Editor page: the review queue with hidden prayer requests (names blurred in the video).                                                        | "Sermons end with prayer requests that name sick and grieving people. They didn't agree to be searchable. Glosa hides those passages until a person decides, and they never reach search or the agent. The AI proposes; a person decides."                                                                                                                   |
| 2:40–2:55 | _Write a study from this answer_: the Markdown draft with linked quotes; print preview.                                                        | "Finally, turn an answer into a study with every quote linked to its moment. From a mountain of video to something you can search, cite and teach from."                                                                                                                                                                                                     |

Close on: **Glosa — Turn footage into footnotes.** Muse Voice Transcribe · Muse Spark · Files API.

## Checklist before recording

- [ ] Written permission from the church to publish the sermons used in the video.
- [ ] Blur every name in the review-queue shots: the prayer requests are private.
- [ ] Rehearse the questions on the deployed demo, so their saved answers are ready if the API is slow.
- [ ] Captions in English for the Spanish parts.
